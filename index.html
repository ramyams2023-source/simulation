Crystal clock pulse generator

HEF4060BP oscillator and 14-stage divider feeding a CD4013BE toggle flip-flop. A 32.768 kHz crystal gives a 1 Hz clock pulse.

Circuit diagram

Crystal X1 sits between CP1 (pin 11) and CP0̄ (pin 9); Rf biases the inverter. Q14 (pin 3) divides the crystal by 16384. The flip-flop has D tied to Q̄, so it toggles on every clock edge and divides by 2 again. Unused CD4013 inputs (SET, RESET) go to ground.
